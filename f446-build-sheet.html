Every pin to wire before the project's own peripherals

J2: SWD header

  1. +3V3
  2. SWDIO (PA13)
  3. SWCLK (PA14)
  4. NRST
  5. GND
  6. SWO (PB3), optional

J3: UART header

  1. UART_TX (PA2)
  2. UART_RX (PA3)
  3. GND

Board TX goes to the adapter's RX, and RX to TX.

Off the chip: USB-C and power

  • CC1 and CC2: each its own 5.1 kΩ to GND
  • VBUS to a 3.3 V regulator that makes +3V3
  • Regulator caps from its datasheet
  • ESD protection on D+, D− and VBUS
