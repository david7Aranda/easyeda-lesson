Pick the HSE frequency

BoardCore clockCrystal
  • Crystal ÷ M gives 2 MHz (ST's recommended PLL input)
  • The PLL reaches the core clock exactly
  • 48 MHz for USB, from PLL Q or (F446) PLLSAI
  • F446 above 168 MHz: over-drive mode enabled

HSE: from the crystal's datasheet

Will it start?

gmcrit = 4 × ESR × (2πF)² × (C0 + CL)²
gmcrit =mA/V

Limit for the F407 and F446 HSE: 1 mA/V.

  • Under the limit (gain margin of 5 or more)

Load capacitors

C1 = C2 = 2 × (CL − Cstray)
2 × (pF − CstraypF ) = each cappF Nearest standardpF, C0G

Example: 8 MHz, ESR 80 Ω, C0 3 pF, CL 20 pF gives 0.43 mA/V (passes). With Cstray 5 pF, each cap is 30 pF.

Limits: F407 0.56 µA/V. F446 0.56 µA/V (low drive) or 1.5 µA/V (high drive).

  • Under the limit (gain margin of 5 or more)

Load capacitors

C1 = C2 = 2 × (CL − Cstray)
2 × (pF − CstraypF ) = each cappF Nearest standardpF, C0G

Example: a common 12.5 pF watch crystal (ESR 70 kΩ, C0 1 pF) gives 2.16 µA/V and fails. A 6 pF, 50 kΩ crystal gives 0.42 µA/V and passes.

Layout checklist

  • Crystal and both caps next to OSC_IN / OSC_OUT
  • Short, roughly equal traces with no vias
  • Caps grounded together near the MCU's VSS
  • Ground guard ring; nothing routed underneath
  • Away from motor drivers and switching regulators
  • Checked in CubeMX's clock tab
